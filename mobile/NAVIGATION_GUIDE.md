# Navigation Guide - 2-Click Rule

The Futures app follows the **2-Click Rule**: Every feature is accessible within **2 clicks or less** from the bottom navigation.

## Bottom Navigation Bar (5 Tabs)

The bottom tab bar is always visible and provides **1-click access** to the 5 core features:

1. **🏠 Home** - Dashboard with quick actions and overview
2. **⛪ Sunday** - Service times, check-in, campus info
3. **💰 Give** - Giving/tithe with Stripe integration
4. **👥 Groups** - Connect groups, browse, join, attendance
5. **🎫 Journey** - Personal spiritual journey, milestones, achievements

## Navigation Map

### Level 1 (Bottom Tabs - 1 Click)
All features accessible directly from tabs:

- **Home** → Dashboard
- **Sunday** → Service & Check-in
- **Give** → Giving/Tithe
- **Groups** → Connect Groups
- **Journey** → Personal Journey

### Level 2 (Within Tabs - 1 Click)
Features accessible from within tab screens:

- **Home** → All features (Sunday, Give, Groups, Journey, Prayer, Events) - 1 click
- **Sunday** → Prayer & Praise - 1 click
- **Groups** → Events - 1 click
- **Journey** → Profile - 1 click

### Level 3 (Secondary Screens - 2 Clicks)
- **Events** - Accessible from Home (1 click) or Groups (1 click)
- **Prayer** - Accessible from Home (1 click) or Sunday (1 click)
- **Profile** - Accessible from Journey (1 click)

## Navigation Examples

### Getting to Give:
1. **Option 1**: Tap "Give" tab (1 click)
2. **Option 2**: Tap "Home" → Tap "Give" card (2 clicks)

### Getting to Events:
1. **Option 1**: Tap "Home" → Tap "Events" card (2 clicks)
2. **Option 2**: Tap "Groups" → Scroll to "Events" button (2 clicks)

### Getting to Prayer:
1. **Option 1**: Tap "Home" → Tap "Prayer" card (2 clicks)
2. **Option 2**: Tap "Sunday" → Scroll to "Prayer & Praise" button (2 clicks)

### Getting to Profile:
1. **Option 1**: Tap "Journey" → Scroll to "Profile & Settings" button (2 clicks)

## Design Principles

1. **Bottom tabs** are always visible (5 max for clarity)
2. **Most-used features** get direct tab access
3. **Secondary features** are accessible within 1 click from any tab
4. **Quick actions** on Home screen provide 1-click access to all major features
5. **Cross-linking** between screens for intuitive navigation

## Visual Hierarchy

- **Primary Navigation**: Bottom tab bar (always visible)
- **Secondary Navigation**: Quick action cards on Home
- **Tertiary Navigation**: Contextual buttons on relevant screens (e.g., Prayer on Sunday screen, Events on Groups screen)

This ensures users can reach any feature quickly without getting lost in deep navigation hierarchies.
