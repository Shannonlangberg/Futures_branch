## Key Differences

### 1. **Additional Content in Go Live Version:**
The Go Live version includes additional text at the end:
- "I press on to take hold of that. For which Christ Jesus took hold of me?"
- "What is that that Christ Jesus took hold of him for?"
- "And what is that that he takes hold of us for?"
- "More more like Jesus and outwork his mission in the earth."
- "I press on to" (incomplete sentence)

### 2. **Text Repetition:**
- The Go Live version repeats "I press on to take hold of that" multiple times
- "What is that" appears multiple times with slight variations

### 3. **Language Differences:**
- **Translated:** "Jesus took hold of me"
- **Go Live:** "Christ Jesus took hold of me" (more formal/full name)

### 4. **Missing from Translated:**
- The entire explanation about what Christ took hold of them for
- The mission statement: "More more like Jesus and outwork his mission in the earth"
- The incomplete final phrase "I press on to"

## Recommendation

The Go Live version appears to be more complete but has:
- Repetitive phrasing that could be cleaned up
- An incomplete sentence at the end ("I press on to")
- Some grammatical issues ("More more like Jesus" - double "more")

The Translated version is cleaner but seems to cut off mid-thought after "The question is, what is that?"
